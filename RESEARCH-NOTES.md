# AI in Education — Maintainer's Research Notes
Last verified: 2026-08-03 (via live browsing). Prices change often — re-verify before publishing.
These notes back the /pages/tools.html and /pages/plans.html pages.

## Frameworks (authoritative sources)
- **TeachAI "AI Guidance for Schools Toolkit"** — organizes guidance around SEVEN Principles for AI
  Guidance: 1) Purpose, 2) Compliance, 3) Knowledge, 4) Balance, 5) Integrity, 6) Agency, 7) Evaluation.
  Partners include UNESCO, ISTE, WEF. Our site's "4 principles" are a distilled, teacher-facing subset.
- **ISTE + ASCD** (iste-ascd.org; iste.org now redirects) — "Profile of an AI-Ready Graduate" with
  ~6 roles (Learner, Researcher, ... Connector, Storyteller). Generative-AI PD guidance available.
- **UNESCO AI Competency Framework** — human-centred, ethics-first competencies for students AND teachers.
- **Common Sense Media** — 9 AI-literacy lessons for grades 6-12; district AI toolkit.
- **Student Privacy** — FERPA (studentprivacy.ed.gov) + Student Privacy Compass (studentprivacycompass.org).

## Consumer AI chatbots (pricing confirmed 2026-08-03)
- **Claude (Anthropic)** — claude.com/pricing. Free $0. Team: $20/seat/mo billed annually, $25 monthly.
  Education plan for institutions (students/faculty/staff, discounted, academic research mode, API credits).
  Anthropic states it does not train on paid conversations; free-tier training stance should be re-verified.
- **Google Gemini / Google AI** — Google AI Plus $9.99/mo (2TB), Google AI Pro $19.99/mo. Workspace for
  Education tiers (free A1 to paid) historically do NOT train on school data — verify for 2026.
  Consumer free Gemini may use data for improvement — assume caution for student data.
- **ChatGPT (OpenAI)** — free/Plus/Team. OpenAI's consumer pricing page was Cloudflare-blocked at research
  time; verify current numbers. General rule: consumer free tiers may train on data; paid plans typically
  don't; ChatGPT for Education / Workspace (school) plans don't train. Treat free consumer ChatGPT as
  NOT safe for student PII.
- **Ollama** — free, local, open models, runs on your own device; data never leaves your machine.
- **Microsoft** — Copilot in Education ~$18/user/mo (from live page). M365 A1 (free) / A3 / A5 plans.
  Schools with M365 Education get some Copilot access via tenant; verify specifics.

## Education-specific AI tools (pricing confirmed 2026-08-03)
- **MagicSchool** — magicschool.ai. Free teacher plan + Plus (paid). Lesson/quiz/feedback generator.
- **Brisk Teaching** — briskteaching.com. Free teacher AI tools + premium (paid) tier.
- **Diffit** — diffit.me. Basic (free) + individual $14.99/mo or $149.99/yr + "Diffit for Schools" (quote).
  Differentiate reading levels/standards.
- **Curipod** — curipod.com. Free plan $0 + paid tiers. Interactive lessons with AI.
- **SchoolAI** — schoolai.com. Free Trial (5 "Space" launches/year), Pro, Scaling (paid). Spaces for students.
- **Khanmigo (Khan Academy)** — khanmigo.ai. **Free for teachers** ("Free, AI-powered teacher assistant").
  Learners ~$4/mo. Tutoring + teacher tools.
- **Canva** — canva.com/education. Historically free for verified educators (Canva for Education).
  Page was Cloudflare-blocked at research time — re-verify. Canva has AI features (Magic Studio).

## Privacy default table (general, for the plans page)
- Free consumer AI (personal account): may train on data → NOT for student PII.
- Paid consumer AI: usually does NOT train → still avoid student PII unless approved.
- School/education plans (Workspace Edu, ChatGPT Edu, M365 Edu, education tools): contractually no-training → safe path for student data.
- Local AI (Ollama): data stays on device → safest.
