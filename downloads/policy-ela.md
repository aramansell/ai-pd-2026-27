## Our stance
AI is a writing tool, not a replacement for a writer. We use it to strengthen the
writing process — generating ideas, organizing thoughts, and getting feedback on
drafts — while holding students responsible for their own words, thinking, and voice.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Brainstorming topic ideas and thesis angles
- Getting feedback on a draft the student has already written
- Checking grammar, punctuation, and style suggestions
- Asking for a clearer explanation of a literary concept
- Outlining or organizing their own ideas

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Writing an essay or assignment in their place
- Copying AI output and passing it off as their own writing
- Substituting AI thinking for the student's own analysis of a text
- Using AI to answer graded assessments or timed writing without permission

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., a brief note: "Used AI to brainstorm ideas"
or "Used AI to proofread this draft"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work or personal details
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- When in doubt about whether AI use is allowed on an assignment, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
