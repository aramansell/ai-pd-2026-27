**School:** Ida B. Wells High School
**Last updated:** [Date]

**Focus:** AI as inspiration, not substitution

## Our stance
AI can be a source of inspiration and a creative partner, but it is not a replacement for the student's own making and expression. Originality and process matter.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Brainstorming ideas and concepts
- Generating references or moodboards for inspiration
- Exploring styles and techniques
- Getting feedback on their own work
- Understanding art or music history

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Submitting AI-generated artwork or music as their own original work
- Copying AI output without significant original contribution
- Using AI to complete graded performance or creation assessments in their place
- Presenting AI work as hand-made

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., \"Used AI to brainstorm this concept\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- Discuss what \"originality\" means in a world with generative tools.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
