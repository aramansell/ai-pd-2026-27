**Focus:** problem-solving must be student-done

## Our stance
AI is a reasoning tool, not an answer machine. Students must be able to do the mathematics themselves; AI can help explain and illuminate, but it never replaces the student's own problem-solving.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Checking the steps of a problem they already solved
- Asking for a step-by-step explanation of a concept
- Generating practice problems on a topic
- Reviewing a concept before a test
- Getting a hint, then trying the problem again themselves

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Entering a problem and copying the answer
- Using AI to complete graded assessments or timed work
- Letting AI solve problems the student cannot solve without it
- Substituting AI for showing their own work and reasoning

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students note it (e.g., \"Used AI to check my steps\" or \"Got a hint on problem 3\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- When in doubt about whether AI is allowed on an assignment, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
