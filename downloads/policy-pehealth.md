**Department:** PE / Health
**School:** Ida B. Wells High School
**Last updated:** [Date]

**Focus:** health information verification and activity design

## Our stance
AI can help design healthy activities and explain wellness concepts, but health decisions must be based on reliable, verifiable information — not AI guesses. Never use AI for personal health advice.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Designing workout or activity routines (under teacher guidance)
- Asking for an explanation of a health or anatomy concept
- Brainstorming wellness project ideas
- Understanding nutrition and fitness principles

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Getting personal medical or health advice
- Inputting personal health information into AI tools
- Copying AI health claims without verifying against reliable sources
- Using AI to answer graded assessments in their place

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., \"Used AI to help plan this routine\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- NEVER input personal health information into AI tools.
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information.
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- Verify health claims against reliable sources.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
