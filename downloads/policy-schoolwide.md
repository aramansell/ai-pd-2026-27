## Our stance
Our school believes AI should be used to support teaching and learning, never to lower the quality of thinking or put student data at risk. This policy is the umbrella; department policies add subject-specific detail.

## Core principles
- AI never lowers cognitive load: it removes grunt work, never does the thinking a student (or teacher) should do.
- AI increases engagement: it's a tool to deepen learning, not a replacement for it.
- We consider bias, race, and inclusion in every AI decision.
- We protect student privacy and ensure equitable access for all students.

## For teachers
- Use AI to save time on planning, feedback, and communication, with professional judgment in charge.
- Verify AI output before using it.
- Never put identifiable student data into tools not approved for student use.
- Follow your department's AI use policy.

## For students
- Use AI as an assistant, never to do your thinking for you.
- Be transparent about when and how you use AI.
- Verify AI output against reliable sources.
- Protect your privacy and others' privacy.

## Privacy & safety
- No identifiable student information (names, grades, IEP/504 details) in unapproved tools.
- Only school-approved tools for student use. See the Free vs Paid Plans page.
- Report any concern to a teacher or administrator.

## Review
This policy will be reviewed and updated at least twice per year as AI evolves.
