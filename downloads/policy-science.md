**School:** Ida B. Wells High School
**Last updated:** [Date]

**Focus:** lab write-ups and data analysis

## Our stance
AI is a tool for investigating and communicating, not for producing conclusions. Students do the thinking, observing, and reasoning; AI can help them write, organize, and understand, but the science is theirs.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Organizing lab notes and observations
- Drafting the structure of a lab report
- Asking for an explanation of a scientific concept
- Checking whether a claim they made is consistent with known science
- Brainstorming hypotheses before testing

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Fabricating data or lab results
- Copying AI explanations as their own analysis
- Using AI to answer graded assessments or write reports in their place
- Presenting AI claims without verifying them against reliable sources

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., \"Used AI to help structure this report\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' data or work
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- Always verify AI claims about scientific facts and processes against a reliable source.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
