**Focus:** source evaluation and historical reasoning

## Our stance
AI can help students understand and organize historical and social ideas, but the ability to evaluate sources and reason about evidence is essential. AI output must be checked against real sources.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Asking for background on a topic or era
- Organizing notes and outlining arguments
- Brainstorming research questions
- Getting a clearer explanation of a concept
- Checking their own draft for clarity

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Copying AI-written essays or answers as their own
- Using fabricated or invented citations from AI (always verify sources)
- Using AI to answer graded assessments in their place
- Treating AI claims as established fact without checking them

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., \"Used AI to outline my essay\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- AI can fabricate citations — always verify sources are real.
- Be alert to bias in AI output and discuss it.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
