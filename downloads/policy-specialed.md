## Our stance
AI can be a powerful accommodation and support for students with disabilities and English learners. We use it to increase access and independence — always protecting privacy and never replacing necessary human support.

## Students MAY use AI for (as an accommodation)
- Text-to-speech / reading support
- Sentence starters and writing scaffolds
- Translation support (with teacher guidance)
- Getting clearer explanations of concepts
- Organizing thoughts before writing

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Using AI to bypass learning the skill being taught
- Copying AI output as their own work
- Sharing IEP, 504, or other private information with AI
- Using AI that is not approved by the school for student use

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used as an accommodation, teachers and students note it in the student's plan or on the assignment, per school guidance.

## Privacy rules (CRITICAL)
- NEVER input IEP/504 details, diagnoses, or identifiable student information into AI tools.
- Use pseudonyms and remove identifying details.
- Only use tools approved by the school for student data (see the Free vs Paid Plans page).

## Notes
- AI should increase access and independence, not replace human support.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
