**Focus:** AI-assisted programming and problem-solving

## Our stance
AI is a powerful tool in technical fields. Students should learn to use it responsibly AND be able to understand and explain the work themselves. AI-assisted is fine; AI-reliant is not.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Getting explanations of code and concepts
- Debugging by asking what might be wrong
- Generating practice problems
- Learning new libraries or syntax
- Getting a starting point, then building understanding

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Copying AI-generated code without understanding what it does
- Using AI to complete graded assessments in their place
- Passing off AI work as entirely their own
- Letting AI do the design and problem-solving thinking for them

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students note it (e.g., \"Used AI to help debug this function\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work or code
- Be careful pasting school code into tools that may store it
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- The most important skill is understanding and explaining your own work.
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
