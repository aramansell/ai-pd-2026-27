**School:** Ida B. Wells High School
**Last updated:** [Date]

**Focus:** translation and language production

## Our stance
The goal of language class is for students to communicate in the target language. AI can support practice, but translation that replaces student production defeats the purpose.

## Students MAY use AI for
- Practicing vocabulary and phrases
- Asking for a sentence model or example
- Getting feedback on their own writing in the target language
- Understanding a grammar concept
- Hearing/reading authentic examples

## Students MAY NOT use AI for
- Translating an entire assignment that the student is meant to write
- Using AI to complete graded assessments or speaking tasks in their place
- Copying AI responses without understanding them
- Substituting AI translation for their own language production

## Crediting AI use
When AI is used, students credit it (e.g., \"Used AI to check my writing\"). Teachers may require this on specific assignments.

## Privacy rules
- Never paste your full name, school, or personal information into AI tools
- Do not input other students' work
- Use only school-approved tools (see the Free vs Paid Plans page)

## Notes
- When in doubt, ASK the teacher first.
- This policy will be reviewed and updated in winter and spring.
