Our shared foundation

Four principles that guide everything here

Every activity in this PD is checked against these principles. They're drawn from current research and guidance from ISTE, TeachAI, UNESCO, and Common Sense Media.

🧠

Never lower cognitive load

AI should remove unnecessary friction (grunt work) so students and teachers can spend their mental energy on important thinking. If an activity lets AI do the thinking a student should be doing, we redesign it — or don't use AI at all.

✨

Always increase engagement

AI is a tool, not the lesson. We use it to spark curiosity, differentiate for readiness, and give students more ways to show what they know — never as a passive screen.

⚖️

Consider bias, race & inclusion

AI models carry real bias, and they can also be a mirror of our assumptions. We name bias openly, use tools that work for every learner, and make sure no student is left behind — or over-relied-upon to "teach the tool."

🛡️

Protect privacy & equity

Student data stays safe: we only put student work into tools that don't train on it, and we make sure the "best" tools don't cost anything, so access is fair for every family.

📚
