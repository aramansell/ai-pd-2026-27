Criterion 1: Voice
  Exceeding: Vivid, personal, and distinctive writing voice throughout.
  Meeting: Clear and personal voice, with some personality.
  Approaching: Voice is present but flat or inconsistent.
  Not yet: No clear voice; writing feels mechanical.

Criterion 2: Details
  Exceeding: Specific, sensory details that bring the experience to life.
  Meeting: Some specific details that support the main idea.
  Approaching: Details are vague or general.
  Not yet: No supporting details.

Criterion 3: Structure
  Exceeding: Clear, effective beginning, middle, and end that build.
  Meeting: Organized and follows a logical sequence.
  Approaching: Somewhat disorganized or hard to follow.
  Not yet: No clear structure.

Criterion 4: Reflection
  Exceeding: Thoughtful reflection on why the experience mattered.
  Meeting: Some reflection on the experience.
  Approaching: Reflection is minimal or surface-level.
  Not yet: No reflection.
