My science fair project tested which soil grows beans the fastest. I used three pots with the same bean seeds and same amount of water, but different soil. I watered them every day and measured them every week for a month. The sandy soil grew the fastest at 12 cm, then potting mix at 9 cm, and clay was slowest at 4 cm. I repeated it twice to make sure. I learned that sandy soil drains best for beans.
