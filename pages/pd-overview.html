PROGRAM OVERVIEW

Three sessions. One shared foundation. All year long.

Three related, 40-minute professional learning sessions for the whole school. Each one builds on the last, every one leaves you with something you can use tomorrow, and all three connect to this year-round resource hub.

⏱ ≈ 40 min each Whole school Free tools 2026–27

The arc

How the three sessions fit together

Session 1 is about you — getting comfortable and productive with AI in your own work. Session 2 is about your classroom — using AI with students, safely and under your direction. Session 3 is about your students — giving them the skills to use AI well themselves.

  1. Session 1 · AI for Your Daily Work

    Focus: Teachers as AI users. Save time on planning, differentiation, rubrics, feedback, and parent communication — while keeping your professional judgment in charge.
    Leave with: 3 ready-to-use AI workflows and a personal prompt toolkit.

    Open Session 1 →

  2. Session 2 · AI With Students in the Classroom

    Focus: Guided, teacher-supervised AI activities that deepen learning — not replace it. We protect cognitive load, engagement, equity, and student data.
    Leave with: 2 adaptable classroom activities and a data-safety checklist.

    Open Session 2 →

  3. Session 3 · Teaching Students to Use AI Well

    Focus: Building student AI literacy — prompt craft, evaluating AI output, ethics and academic honesty, and recognizing bias.
    Leave with: A ready-to-teach AI literacy mini-lesson and an honest-work class agreement.

    Open Session 3 →

The format

What every session looks like

⏱ The 40 minutes

A tight agenda: warm-up (5 min), learn (10 min), hands-on practice (15 min), share & plan (10 min). No death-by-slides — you'll be working in the tools by minute ten.

Go deeper

Each session links to extended reading, self-paced practice, and coaching resources for anyone who wants to go beyond the 40 minutes.

Tangible takeaways

Every session ends with something concrete you can use immediately: a prompt bank, an activity, a checklist, a template. You never leave empty-handed.

Department follow-through

Each session pairs with department activities so the learning continues in your own meetings all year — not just on PD days.

The lens we use for everything

Every activity is checked against these four principles

Never lower cognitive load

AI removes grunt work; it never does the thinking a student (or you) should be doing.

Always increase engagement

AI is a spark, not the lesson. It deepens curiosity and gives more ways to show learning.

Consider bias, race & inclusion

We name bias, design for every learner, and never let a tool reproduce inequity.

Protect privacy & equity

Student data stays safe, and the best tools cost nothing so every family has equal access.
