FREE vs WORKSPACE vs PAID

What student work can go where?

The most important question in AI + school isn't "which tool is best" — it's "where can student work safely go?" This page breaks down free, Workspace (school), and paid plans — and which ones are safe for student data.

💰 Free 🏫 Workspace / school 💳 Paid 🔒 Safety matters most
Safe approved for student work Caution teacher use only (no student PII) Risk keep student data out Free Paid Freemium Edu plan

Read this first

The single most useful idea on this page

🔑
Free ≠ safe for student data

Free consumer accounts often train on your data. A paid or school plan usually doesn't. So the safest place for student work is almost always a school / Workspace / education plan — not a personal free account, even if it's the "best" tool.

💰

Free plans

Great for teachers' own work and learning. But free consumer tiers may train on your chats — keep student work out of them.

🏫

Workspace / school plans

The safest place for student work. Contractually protected from training. This is where student-facing AI belongs.

💳

Paid personal plans

Usually don't train on your data. Fine for teachers. Still avoid student PII unless the plan is specifically approved for it.

Your default

A quick decision rule for student work

  1. Is it student work (could identify a student)?

    If it contains names, grades, IEP details, or anything personal — it goes only in an approved school/Workspace/edu plan.

  2. Is it your own professional work?

    Lesson plans, brainstorming, rubrics without student names — free and paid personal tools are fine (still remove names to be safe).

  3. When in doubt?

    Use the school/Workspace account, use a local tool (Ollama), or remove identifying info. Ask before experimenting.

Why this matters

Why school plans are the safe default for student data

🤝
There's a contract (DPA)

School plans have a data-processing agreement. That's what makes student data legally safe.

🚫
No training on your data

School/edu plans are contractually set to NOT train on your data. Free consumer tiers don't promise this.

💳
Free for the school

Many edu plans (Canva for Education, Khanmigo for teachers, Workspace A1) are free for schools — great for equity.

⚖️
Equal access

Using school-provided, free tools means every student participates regardless of what their family can pay for.

Equity & families

A note on equity

If some families buy premium AI for their kids and others can't, that's a real equity gap. We recommend:

  • Use free, school-provided tools as the baseline for classroom work.
  • Don't require any paid tool for homework.
  • Tell families which tools you use and that school accounts protect student data.
