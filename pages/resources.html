The PPS AI Guidebook

This site follows the PPS AI Guidebook, the district's authoritative guidance for staff, students, and families. It covers the vision, staff responsibilities, AI Levels of Use for students, data privacy & security (FERPA, COPPA, CIPA, OSIPA), academic integrity, environmental impact, deepfakes, and approved tools. When this site and the Guidebook ever differ, the Guidebook wins.

The pedagogy

Cognitive load & effective AI pedagogy

Why we emphasize cognitive load: AI can quietly shift the thinking from student to machine. These resources help you design activities where the student stays the thinker.

The design question to always ask

"Who is doing the important thinking?" If the AI produces the answer and the student copies it, cognitive load drops in the wrong direction. Good AI activities keep the student's brain on.

Stay current

Keeping up as AI changes

AI tools change fast. Here's how to stay reasonably current without burning out:

  1. Check this site's Toolbox & Plans pages

    We keep the tool comparisons and free vs paid info updated.

  2. Follow the framework orgs

    ISTE, TeachAI, and Common Sense publish updates as tools evolve.

  3. Use your department

    Your monthly department activities are a built-in way to keep skills current together.

  4. Try one new thing per term

    Don't chase every update. Pick one new workflow or tool per term and master it.
